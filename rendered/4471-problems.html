The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Tenants that write the day first get day and month swapped: “8 January landed on 1 August”. Every tenant on file is day first, so every tenant has it, through every channel.

hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Alder Physio, Copper Studio and Pale Harbour run booking-v3. Their people type what the hint tells them, month first, and the form reads it the tenant’s way, day first. The swap looks the same as parser, but the fix is in the form, and the parser fix does not touch it.

one-day-move: the date moves one day back, not day for month. “Tuesday 6 October showed as Monday.” “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” An invite arrived before the booking. This is a time zone fault, not a parse order fault. The desk saw it (desk-notes.json), but the cause table files these reports under parser by tenant, so they vanish into the biggest row.

Why #4471 looked like one issue

  • The desk merges on the subject line (decision 0001). All three problems arrive as “wrong day” or “wrong date”, so they merged into one ticket.
  • Intake tags a report from the reporter’s browser. The cause depends on the tenant: its locale and its booking form. The column “tagged otherwise by intake” counts where the two disagree.
  • The export comes in pages that overlap, so some reports come twice. Count by id.

What to do

  1. Fix the parser to read dates in the tenant’s locale (ticket for parser).
  2. Make booking-v3 print the hint in the tenant’s order, and read what it prints (ticket for hint).
  3. Find where a booking time turns into a date without its time zone (ticket for one-day-move).
  4. Do not mark #4471 known until all three tickets close.
